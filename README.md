One funny line, at most 200 characters, roasting the fridge — an entry for make.fun's One Line, One Laugh: Roast Your Fridge bounty ($3, top 3 paid $1 each). Round 2 entry: this round actually roasts the fridge itself (round 1 roasted a phone instead).

## Entry

> My fridge hums at 3am like it's gossiping about my midnight snacks, then goes dead silent the second I open the door.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) reads the line from `entry.txt` and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
